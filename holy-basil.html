Tulsi is known as the “Queen of Herbs” in Ayurveda for its adaptogenic, antiviral, and antibacterial effects. Used in herbal teas, immunity blends, skin care, and natural remedies, JontyMart offers clean, shade-dried leaves with optimal potency.
