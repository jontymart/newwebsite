About Bengal Gram (Chana)

Bengal Gram is also known as Desi Chana or Kala Chana. It is a small, dark brown pulse that is packed with protein, dietary fiber, iron, and vitamins. It is mainly used in Indian cuisine for curries, flours, and snacks. Our chana is naturally grown, machine-cleaned, and hygienically packed for export markets.

Key Features

  • Available in split, whole, and flour forms
  • Natural and chemical-free produce

Applications

  • Used in dishes, snacks, and besan (gram flour)
  • Essential in vegetarian and vegan meals
  • Commonly exported to Middle East, UK, and Australia
