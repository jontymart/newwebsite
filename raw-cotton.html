About Raw Cotton

India is one of the largest producers of raw cotton. JontyMart exports 100% Indian origin cotton lint in compressed bales, ready for use in yarn spinning. Our cotton is moisture-controlled, clean, and free from foreign matter.

Key Features

  • Staple Length: 28mm–32mm
  • Micronaire: 3.8 to 4.5 (avg)
  • Strength: High, suitable for ring and rotor spinning
  • Moisture content under 8%
  • Packaging: Compressed & strapped bales of ~170 kg

Applications

  • Spinning into yarn (for fabric and garment industries)
  • Used by mills across Bangladesh, Vietnam, Turkey, China
  • Ideal for contract and bulk cotton buyers

Storage Instructions

Store in dry, covered storage spaces away from moisture and direct sunlight. Use within 12 months for best results.
