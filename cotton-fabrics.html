About Cotton Fabrics

JontyMart supplies 100% pure cotton woven and knitted fabrics that are breathable, soft, and suitable for various applications. From bedsheets to fashion apparel, our range caters to bulk buyers worldwide.

Key Features

  • Available in plain, printed, dyed, and yarn-dyed varieties
  • High GSM consistency and durability
  • Pre-shrunk and reactive dyed options
  • Customizable widths: 44”, 58”, and 108”

Applications

  • Used in home textiles, garments, uniforms, quilting, and crafts
  • Ideal for wholesale export orders
  • Trusted by buyers in UAE, Australia, UK, and USA

Why Choose JontyMart?

  • Multiple fabric finishes: mercerized, brushed, and peach finish
  • Direct mill sourcing from Tamil Nadu and Gujarat
  • Packaging in rolls and folds as per export standard
  • Assistance with international documentation and logistics

Care Instructions

Machine wash cold with similar colors. Tumble dry low. Iron as needed. Avoid bleaching to preserve color richness.
