About Pillow Covers

Our pillow covers are stitched using high-quality cotton fabric sourced from Indian mills. They come in standard and custom sizes, featuring premium zippers or flap closures for long-lasting comfort and elegance.

Key Features

  • 100% cotton, soft-touch finish
  • Size options: 16x16, 18x18, 20x30 inches
  • Available in plain, printed, embroidered, or block-print
  • Colorfast and machine washable
  • Packed in eco-friendly polybags or boxes

Applications

  • Used in bedding sets, hotel decor, and home furnishings
  • Popular with interior designers, wholesalers, and gift brands
  • Custom branding and embroidery options available

Why Choose JontyMart?

  • In-house QC and export packing
  • Low MOQ for trial shipments
  • Fast delivery to Australia, Europe, and Gulf regions
  • Support for bulk and mixed-style orders

Storage Instructions

Store folded in dry, sealed packaging. Keep away from moisture, dampness, and sunlight. Wash in cold water before first use for best feel.
