About PVC Rexine

PVC Rexine is a type of faux leather made by applying a PVC coating to a fabric base. It mimics real leather’s look and feel while being more affordable and versatile. JontyMart offers export-quality PVC Rexine in multiple textures and colors.

Key Features

  • Waterproof & Easy to Clean: Great for daily-use products and outdoor furniture.
  • Durable & Scratch-Resistant: Designed to last longer in demanding applications.
  • Affordable: Budget-friendly alternative to natural leather.
  • Customizable: Available in matte, glossy, textured, and printed finishes.
  • Eco Variants Available: Low-VOC and lead-free options for sustainability.

Applications

  • Automobile interiors and seat covers
  • Office and home furniture upholstery
  • Bags, wallets, and school items
  • Footwear linings and straps
  • Packaging and display items

Care Instructions

Clean with a damp cloth using mild soap. Avoid exposure to sharp objects and prolonged heat. Store in cool, dry conditions for best longevity.
