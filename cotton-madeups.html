About Cotton Made-ups

Our cotton made-ups are value-added fabric products used in homes, hotels, and cafes. These include utility and decorative items manufactured in bulk for both B2B and retail export markets.

Products Offered

  • Table Cloths and Napkins
  • Kitchen Towels and Aprons
  • Curtains and Window Drapes
  • Cushion and Pillow Covers
  • Quilts, Duvet Covers, and Throws

Key Features

  • 100% cotton or cotton-linen blends
  • Available in yarn-dyed, printed, or embroidered finishes
  • Size, pattern, and stitching customizable
  • Eco-friendly and OEKO-TEX certified options

Why Choose JontyMart?

  • High-quality stitching and finishing
  • Bulk capacity with packaging and tagging
  • Fast production lead time and exports
  • Low MOQ for custom orders

Storage Instructions

Keep products folded in clean, moisture-free environments. Wash in cold water before first use. Iron on low heat if needed.
