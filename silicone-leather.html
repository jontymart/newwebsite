About Silicone Leather

Silicone Leather is made by bonding silicone with fabric layers to create a superior alternative to PU or PVC leather. It offers unmatched performance in healthcare, marine, auto, aviation, and children’s products due to its non-toxic, hypoallergenic nature.

Key Features

  • Extreme Durability: UV, fire, and abrasion resistant.
  • Eco-Friendly: Free of PVC, phthalates, and heavy metals.
  • Hygienic & Non-Toxic: Safe for hospitals, schools, and baby products.
  • Stain & Chemical Resistant: Easy to clean and disinfect.
  • Wide Operating Temp: Tolerates -50°C to 200°C.

Applications

  • Marine and outdoor upholstery
  • Hospital beds and stretchers
  • Premium car seats and dashboards
  • Baby strollers and carriers
  • Flame-retardant furniture

Care Instructions

Wipe clean with soft cloth and mild soap. Avoid sharp objects. No bleaching required. Store in a cool, dry area away from oils and inks.
