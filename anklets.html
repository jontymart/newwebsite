About Anklets

Our anklets are handcrafted in India using traditional and modern techniques. Whether you're walking at a wedding or just dancing barefoot, they add flair and beauty to your feet.

Key Features

  • Material: Oxidized metal, silver-plated, beads, brass
  • Designs: Ghungroo, tribal, stone, minimalist, layered
  • Closure: Lobster clasp or adjustable chain
  • Size: Standard free-size for adults

Applications

  • Perfect for festivals, weddings, and casual fashion
  • Best seller for women, girls, and dance groups
  • Ideal for fashion gift boxes and resellers

  • Private label and custom design options
  • Affordable pricing and reliable shipping

Care Instructions

Keep away from water and chemicals. Store in a dry pouch. Clean gently with a soft cloth to retain shine and color.
