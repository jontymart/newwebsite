About PU Leather

PU Leather is a synthetic material made by coating a split leather or fabric base with a layer of polyurethane. It is designed to deliver a leather-like feel and appearance without using animal hide, making it a popular choice for eco-conscious buyers.

Key Features

  • Soft & Lightweight: Easy to work with and comfortable for end users.
  • Cost-Effective: More affordable than genuine leather with similar appeal.
  • Custom Finishes: Available in matte, glossy, embossed, and printed textures.
  • Low Maintenance: Easy to clean and resistant to cracking and peeling.
  • Animal-Free: 100% vegan and cruelty-free material.

Applications

  • Fashion jackets, handbags, and wallets
  • Upholstery and automotive interiors
  • Footwear and belt linings
  • Diaries, planners, and office accessories
  • Retail displays and packaging

Care Instructions

Wipe regularly with a clean, damp cloth. Do not expose to excessive heat or harsh chemicals. Store rolls in a dry place away from sunlight for best durability.
