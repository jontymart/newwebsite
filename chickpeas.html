About Chickpeas (Kabuli Chana)

Kabuli Chana, also known as white chickpeas or garbanzo beans, are globally used in salads, curries, hummus, and snacks. They are protein-rich, high in dietary fiber, and ideal for gluten-free and vegan diets. JontyMart delivers top-grade Kabuli chickpeas sorted, cleaned, and packed under international food standards.

Key Features

  • Large-sized, round, and creamy-white beans
  • Rich in protein, iron, and folate
  • Available in polished and unpolished varieties
  • 100% natural, free from artificial additives
  • Custom bulk and retail packaging

Applications

  • Used in hummus, salads, curries, and snacks
  • Popular in Middle Eastern, Mediterranean, and Indian cuisines
  • Great choice for health food and vegan industries

Why Choose JontyMart?

  • Direct procurement from premium Indian farms
  • High-quality processing with modern technology
  • Attractive pricing for importers and distributors
  • Flexible shipping & documentation support

Storage Instructions

Store in airtight containers in a cool and dry place. Best before 12–15 months from the date of packaging. Avoid moisture exposure.
