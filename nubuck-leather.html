About Nubuck Leather

Nubuck leather is crafted from the outer side of the hide (top grain), which is sanded or buffed to produce a fine, velvety surface. It is stronger and thicker than suede, with a luxurious nap and a sophisticated look. Our nubuck is carefully processed to ensure a consistent, soft touch and rich color.

Key Features

  • Top Grain Quality: Made from the strongest part of the hide for superior durability.
  • Velvety Surface: Soft, matte finish with a subtle nap.
  • Elegant Appearance: Looks premium and feels luxurious.
  • Breathable & Comfortable: Ideal for shoes, jackets, and upholstery.
  • Custom Colors & Finishes: Available in a variety of shades and thicknesses.

Applications

  • Premium shoes and boots
  • Luxury handbags and wallets
  • Jackets and outerwear
  • Upholstery and furniture
  • Fashion accessories

Care Instructions

Use a nubuck brush to maintain the nap and remove dirt. Protect from water and stains with a nubuck protector spray. Avoid direct sunlight and store in a cool, dry place. For deep cleaning, consult a leather care specialist.
