About Neem Leaves

Neem (Azadirachta indica) is a powerful plant native to India. The leaves are traditionally used in Ayurveda for their anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and immune-boosting properties. Our leaves are carefully harvested, shade-dried, and packed for maximum potency.
