About Pigeon Peas (Toor/Arhar)

Pigeon Peas, also known as Toor or Arhar Dal, are one of the most consumed lentils in India. They are an excellent source of plant protein, iron, and dietary fiber. Our export-grade pigeon peas are processed with the latest technology and are free from any artificial polish or additives.
