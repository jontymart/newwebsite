About Black Pepper

Black Pepper is a universal spice with pungent flavor and medicinal value. Our export-grade pepper is cleaned, graded, and packed in hygienic facilities. It’s ideal for spice companies, wholesalers, and food industries globally.

Key Features

  • High piperine content and essential oil retention
  • Uniform bold berries with natural shine
  • 100% sun-dried and unpolished
  • Available in whole, crushed, and powder form
  • Meets international food safety standards

Applications

  • Used in spice mixes, sauces, and meat rubs
  • Popular in Indian, continental, and global cuisines
  • Medicinal use in Ayurveda and natural remedies

Why Choose JontyMart?

  • Sourced from Kerala and Tamil Nadu farms
  • Flexible pack sizes and private label options
  • Export support with timely dispatch
  • Affordable rates for bulk shipments

Storage Instructions

Keep in dry and airtight containers. Avoid exposure to moisture and direct heat. Best before 18–24 months from packaging date.
