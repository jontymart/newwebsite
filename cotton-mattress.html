About Cotton Mattress (Gadda)

These thick, traditional mattresses are made by layering pure cotton within high-quality cotton fabric. Ideal for floor use or on wooden beds, they provide natural insulation and sleeping comfort.

Key Features

  • Handcrafted using 100% natural cotton
  • Available in single, queen, and king sizes
  • Outer fabric: cotton canvas, mulmul, or printed cover
  • Thickness: 2" to 5" as per order

Applications

  • Used in homes, dharamshalas, temples, and lodges
  • Traditional bedding option for Indian-style homes
  • Popular for export to Indian diaspora overseas

Care Instructions

Dry under sunlight regularly to maintain fluffiness. Avoid soaking or machine washing. Use a cotton mattress cover for longer lifespan.
