About Suede Leather

Suede leather is made from the underside of animal hide, giving it a soft, napped finish. It is lighter and more flexible than full-grain leather, making it a popular choice for fashion and accessories. Our suede is carefully processed to ensure a uniform, velvety texture and vibrant color.

Key Features

  • Soft & Velvety Texture: Luxurious feel and elegant appearance.
  • Flexible & Lightweight: Comfortable for garments and accessories.
  • Fashionable: Available in a wide range of colors and thicknesses.
  • Breathable: Allows air circulation, ideal for shoes and jackets.
  • Custom Finishes: Can be embossed or printed for unique designs.

Applications

  • Fashion shoes and boots
  • Jackets and coats
  • Handbags and purses
  • Gloves and hats
  • Upholstery and home décor
  • Crafts and accessories

Care Instructions

Use a suede brush to maintain the nap and remove dirt. Protect from water and stains with a suede protector spray. Avoid direct sunlight and store in a cool, dry place. For deep cleaning, consult a leather care specialist.
