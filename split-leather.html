Split Leather
Split Leather is the lower layer of the hide, separated from the top grain during processing. It is economical, versatile, and durable, making it ideal for work gloves, linings, suede, and budget-friendly leather goods. About Split Leather

Split leather is produced by splitting the hide into layers. The top layer becomes grain leather, while the lower layer is called split leather. It is more affordable than full grain or top grain leather, yet still offers good durability and versatility for a wide range of uses.

Key Features

  • Economical: Cost-effective alternative to top grain leather, ideal for budget-conscious buyers.
  • Versatile: Can be finished as suede, coated, or embossed for various applications.
  • Durable: Offers good strength and wear resistance for many products.
  • Custom Finishes: Available in suede, pigmented, or PU-coated finishes.
  • Consistent Quality: Processed to meet international export standards.

Applications

  • Work gloves and safety gear
  • Shoe linings and insoles
  • Budget handbags and wallets
  • Book bindings and covers
  • Furniture and automotive linings
  • Suede products and more

Care Instructions

Clean with a soft brush or cloth. For suede finish, use a suede brush to maintain texture. Avoid excessive moisture and direct sunlight. For coated split leather, wipe with a damp cloth and let air dry.
