About Cotton Quilts

Our Indian-style Rajai quilts are made from layered cotton sheets filled with fluffy cotton inside, stitched carefully to ensure warmth, breathability, and long-lasting comfort. Perfect for chilly weather or AC rooms.

Key Features

  • Available in king, queen, and single sizes
  • Filled with 100% carded cotton
  • Outer fabric in cotton voile, mulmul, or printed fabric
  • Hand or machine stitched for firmness

Applications

  • Used in homes, hotels, hostels, and as gifts
  • Preferred in cold regions like North India, Nepal, Europe
  • Also used in boutique bedding exports

Washing Instructions

Dry clean recommended. If washing at home, use mild detergent and air dry completely. Avoid soaking or wringing to protect stitching and cotton filling.
