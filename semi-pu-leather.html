About Semi PU Leather

Semi PU Leather is a hybrid synthetic leather combining a PVC base with a polyurethane (PU) topcoat. This results in an economical product that maintains flexibility, durability, and visual appeal. It is highly favored by manufacturers of mid-range accessories and furniture.

Key Features

  • Balanced Cost & Performance: Lower cost than full PU, better finish than PVC alone.
  • Good Stretch & Flexibility: Suitable for stitched and curved applications.
  • Textured Finishes: Available in leather grain, embossed, glossy, and more.
  • Durable Surface: Resistant to scuffs, moisture, and abrasion.
  • Various Colors: Custom tones and roll sizes available for bulk buyers.

Applications

  • Shoes and sandals
  • Laptop bags and fashion backpacks
  • Office and restaurant furniture
  • Wallets, belts, and fashion accessories
  • Budget automotive seat covers

Care Instructions

Wipe with a dry or slightly damp cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals. For best results, keep away from direct sunlight and store in cool, dry places.
